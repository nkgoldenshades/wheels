1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Data Type Examples Purpose

4. Data Security

4.1 How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Control: Only authorized employees can access personal data
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing (we don't store full card details)
  • Password Hashing: Passwords stored using bcrypt hashing
  • Secure Servers: Hosted on Firebase and Supabase with enterprise-grade security
  • Regular Audits: Security reviews and vulnerability scans

4.2 Your Responsibility

  • Keep your password secure and don't share it
  • Log out from shared devices
  • Enable two-factor authentication (if available)
  • Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access
⚠️ No Security is Perfect: While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Use VaultWheels at your own risk.

5. GPS & Location Tracking

5.1 When We Track Your Location

  • During Active Bookings: GPS tracked for navigation, safety, and verification
  • For Search: Current location used to show nearby vehicles (optional)
  • For Photos: Location embedded in photos for refund verification

5.2 When We DON'T Track

  • When you're not using the app (no background tracking)
  • When you're logged out
  • When location permissions are disabled

5.3 Control Your Location

  • Deny location permission (some features may not work)
  • Enable only while using the app
  • View location history in account settings
  • Request deletion of location data

13. Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights after death
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

14. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU Users)

If you're in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to be forgotten
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

15. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it's used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights
