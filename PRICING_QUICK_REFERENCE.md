## 📊 Affordability Formula

```
Affordability % = (Monthly Subscription / Average Monthly Income) × 100

Target Ranges:
✅ Tier 1: 0.2% - 0.5% (very affordable)
✅ Tier 2: 2% - 5% (affordable)
✅ Tier 3: 3% - 8% (accessible)
```

### Real Examples:

| Country | Avg Income | Premium Cost | % of Income | Status |
|---------|-----------|--------------|-------------|--------|
| 🇺🇸 USA | $4,000 | $12.99 | 0.32% | ✅ Very affordable |
| 🇮🇳 India | ₹40,000 | ₹999 | 2.5% | ✅ Affordable |
| 🇵🇰 Pakistan | Rs 50,000 | Rs 1,665 | 3.3% | ✅ Accessible |

## 🔍 How to Determine a Country's Tier

### Step 1: Check GDP per capita
- **$40,000+** → Tier 1
- **$5,000 - $20,000** → Tier 2
- **$1,000 - $5,000** → Tier 3

### Step 2: Verify with local market
- What do Netflix, LinkedIn, Amazon Prime charge?
- Is our price competitive?

### Step 3: Calculate affordability
- Is it 0.2% - 8% of average monthly income?
- If yes ✅, tier is correct
- If no ❌, adjust tier
