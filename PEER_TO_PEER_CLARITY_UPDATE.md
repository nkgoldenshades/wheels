### **Section 4: Payments - Peer-to-Peer Contributions**

**Critical Statement:**
> "VaultWheels does NOT provide services or rent vehicles. Users transact with each other."

**How It Works:**
- User A (Helper) offers to User B (Seeker)
- User B pays User A (we just facilitate payment)
- Platform takes 5% for providing technology

**Important Clarifications:**
- "Payment is between users, NOT between you and VaultWheels"
- "VaultWheels is NOT the service provider or vehicle owner"
- "We only facilitate payment processing (like PayPal, Venmo)"
- "Security deposits are decided by users, not by us"

**Why We're Not Liable:**
- We don't own, operate, or provide vehicles
