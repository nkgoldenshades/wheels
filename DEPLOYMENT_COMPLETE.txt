Fix: Vehicle list navigation - filter by profile_id at database level 
     and add timeout to slot queries

  profile_id at database level

- Added 10-second timeout to getAllSlotsForVehicle() in both SlotRepository 
  and CustomerSlotRepository
- Prevents vehicle detail screen from hanging indefinitely
