## 5. Document Storage & Retention

### 5.1 Storage Method

- Documents are **encrypted at rest** (AES-256)
- Stored on **secure cloud servers** in India
- Protected with **SSL/TLS encryption** in transit
- Access restricted to authorized personnel only

### 5.2 Retention Period

**Identity Documents:**
- Retained for **30 days** after verification
- Auto-deleted after retention period
- Users can request immediate deletion

**Profile Data:**
- Retained as long as account is active
- Deleted upon account closure + 30 days

**Transaction Data:**
- Retained for **7 years** (legal requirement)
- Anonymized after 1 year

### 5.3 Verification Methods

Users have two choices:

#### Option A: AI-Assisted Backend Verification
- Documents uploaded to secure backend
- AI verifies identity automatically
- Documents encrypted and stored temporarily
- Faster verification (instant to 24 hours)
- Auto-deleted after 30 days

**Consent Required:** ✓ User must consent to temporary storage

#### Option B: Local Verification (Privacy First)
- Documents stored **locally on your device only**
- No data sent to backend
- Manual verification by team (24-48 hours)
- Maximum privacy
- No permanent storage

**Consent Required:** ✓ User confirms local-only storage
