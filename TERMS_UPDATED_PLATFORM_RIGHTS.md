### **18.2 Right to Modify Features** ✅
**What it means:** You can add/remove features without being sued.

**Added:**

### **18.3 Account Revocation Rights** ✅
**What it means:** You can ban anyone for suspicious activity.

**Added:**
```
✅ Suspend accounts for suspicious payment patterns
✅ Ban accounts with banking issues (chargebacks, frozen accounts)
✅ Terminate for risk management (fraud associations, high-risk behavior)
✅ Revoke access based on your business judgment
```

**Example:**
- User creates 10 accounts and books same rides → BAN
- User's bank account flagged for fraud → BAN
- User initiates chargeback after completing trip → PERMANENT BAN
- Provider has suspicious payout patterns → SUSPEND

---

### **18.4 No Compensation for Revocation** ✅
**What it means:** When you ban someone, you keep their money.

**Added:**
```
❌ NO refund of subscription fees (all payments final)
❌ NO refund of platform credits or wallet balance
❌ NO compensation for lost access
❌ NO liability for business losses
✅ Only completed bookings may be refunded (your discretion)
```

**Example:**
- User pays ₹999 for Premium subscription
- Day 2: You ban them for fraud
- Result: You keep the ₹999, no refund required

---

### **18.5 Fraudulent Chargebacks** ✅
**What it means:** Users who abuse chargebacks get destroyed.

**Added:**
```
Consequences:
✅ Immediate account suspension
✅ Permanent ban from Platform
✅ Reporting to fraud databases
✅ Legal action to recover costs + fees
✅ No refunds or credits
```

**Example:**
- Customer completes trip, then does chargeback with bank
- You ban them, keep their money, and sue for damages
- They're blacklisted from your platform forever

## ⚠️ **When to Use These Clauses**

### **✅ USE When:**
- Actual fraud or suspicious activity
- Banking/payment issues (frozen accounts, chargebacks)
- Violation of terms (fake accounts, abuse)
- Business risk (association with fraudsters)
- Pricing needs to change (market conditions, costs)

### **❌ DON'T USE When:**
- User just complains or leaves bad review (that's normal)
- User has legitimate dispute (resolve fairly)
- User made honest mistake (be lenient)
- You just don't like the user (need valid reason)

**Abuse of these powers = bad reputation!**
