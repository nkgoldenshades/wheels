# Subscription Pricing Tiers - Economic Explanation

## Overview

Pricing is determined by **each country's economic tier**, NOT by continent. Continents are only used for code organization. The tier assignment is based on:

1. **GDP per capita** (purchasing power)
2. **Average income levels**
3. **Digital service affordability**
4. **Market standards** for similar services

## How Tier Assignment Works

### Economic Indicators Used:

1. **World Bank GDP per capita data**
2. **IMF purchasing power parity (PPP) data**
3. **Local SaaS pricing benchmarks**
4. **Digital service adoption rates**

### Examples of Tier Assignment:

#### Why is UAE Tier 1 but Egypt Tier 2?
- 🇦🇪 UAE: GDP per capita $43,000 → Tier 1
- 🇪🇬 Egypt: GDP per capita $3,600 → Tier 2

#### Why is Poland Tier 2 but Germany Tier 1?
- 🇵🇱 Poland: GDP per capita $18,000 → Tier 2
- 🇩🇪 Germany (EUR): GDP per capita $51,000 → Tier 1

#### Why is Vietnam Tier 3 but Thailand Tier 2?
- 🇻🇳 Vietnam: GDP per capita $4,100 → Tier 3
- 🇹🇭 Thailand: GDP per capita $7,800 → Tier 2

## Expert Plan Deposit Explained

### Why Different Deposit Amounts?

The **refundable deposit** scales with the tier to maintain **equivalent purchasing power**:

| Tier | Deposit Amount | Equivalent to | Purpose |
|------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | $599 | ~2 weeks salary | Legal protection fund |
| 2 | ₹50,000 (~$600) | ~1.5 months salary | Legal protection fund |
| 3 | ₹25,000 (~$300) | ~1.5 months salary | Legal protection fund |

**Why this matters:**
- Deposit represents **similar financial commitment** across all tiers
- Used for legal disputes/jurisdiction issues
- Refunded after 12 months of good standing
- Acts as quality filter for serious partners

## Regional Pricing Strategy

### Why Not One Global Price?

❌ **One global price ($12.99) would mean:**
- Affordable in USA (0.3% of income)
- Expensive in India (2.7% of income)
- **Unaffordable in Nigeria (13% of income)** ❌

✅ **Tiered pricing means:**
- Fair affordability across all markets
- Maximizes market penetration
- Respects local purchasing power
- Competitive with local alternatives

## How to Verify Affordability

### Quick Check Formula:

```
Affordability Score = (Monthly Subscription / Average Monthly Income) × 100

Target Range:
- Tier 1: 0.2% - 0.5% (very affordable)
- Tier 2: 2% - 5% (affordable)
- Tier 3: 3% - 8% (accessible)
```
