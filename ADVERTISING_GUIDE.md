- Providers (drivers, delivery persons) post available slots

## 📊 Ad Campaign Strategy

### Phase 1: Awareness (Weeks 1-4)
**Goal**: Get 10,000 downloads
**Channels**: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
**Budget**: ₹50,000
**Message**: "The flexible marketplace for rides"
**Target**: Urban professionals, students, part-time drivers

### Phase 2: Conversion (Weeks 5-8)
**Goal**: 5,000 active users booking
**Channels**: Google Ads, Email, In-app
**Budget**: ₹75,000
**Message**: "Complete your first booking, earn rewards"
**Target**: Downloaded but inactive users

### Phase 3: Retention (Weeks 9-12)
**Goal**: 70% monthly active users
**Channels**: Email, Push notifications, Referral
**Budget**: ₹50,000
**Message**: "Refer friends, earn together"
**Target**: Active users

## 🎨 Ad Creative Variations

### A/B Test 1: Price vs. Convenience
**Variation A**: "Rides from ₹50"
**Variation B**: "Book in 30 seconds"
**Winner**: Usually Variation B (convenience resonates better)

### A/B Test 2: Trust vs. Earning
**Variation A**: "Verified drivers with ratings"
**Variation B**: "Earn ₹2000+/day"
**Winner**: Depends on audience (students prefer earning, professionals prefer trust)

### A/B Test 3: Problem vs. Solution
**Variation A**: "Tired of waiting for cabs?"
**Variation B**: "Your ride awaits - Book now"
**Winner**: Usually Problem-Solution (emotional connection)
