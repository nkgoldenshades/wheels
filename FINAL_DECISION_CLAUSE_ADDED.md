- Freedom of Contract (Indian Contract Act, 1872)

- Industry standard practice
- Enforceable as long as not against public policy

## ⚠️ Important Limits

### **What "Final Decision" CANNOT Override:**

❌ **Consumer Protection Laws**
- If government mandates refund, you must comply
- Can't violate statutory consumer rights

❌ **Criminal Activity**
- If user commits crime, police/courts decide (not you)

❌ **Fraud Against You**
- If user defrauds you, you can still sue them

❌ **Constitutional Rights**
- Can't violate fundamental rights

**But for 99% of disputes, your decision IS final!** ✅
